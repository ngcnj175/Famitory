# 9. Share (SHARE)

Press the **SHARE** button to turn your game into a URL.
Share that URL and anyone can play your game!

<img src="assets/screenshots/シェアモーダル.png" alt="Share dialog" style="max-width:50%; min-width:260px; display:block; margin:16px 0; border:none; box-shadow:none; border-radius:0;">

---

## Allow Remix

A remix is a new game built on top of someone else's game, freely rearranged.
Check **Allow Remix** and your game will be released as "OK to remix," so anyone can build on it.

---

## Register to ARCADE

Check **Register to ARCADE** and your game will appear in the public gallery on the ARCADE screen. A chance for strangers to find and play your game!

- **Comment** … A short description of your game
- **Thumbnail** … The image shown in the game list. Use the "Capture from Play" button to take a screenshot straight from the play screen. (You need to play the game once first.)

---

## Publishing your game

### Before you publish
Open the PLAY screen and make sure the title and creator name are set. Those are the names that get published.

### How to share
Once published, pick your favorite way to share from the share screen!

- **Copy URL** … Paste it into chat, email, or DMs
- **Post on X** … Open the X (formerly Twitter) post screen (with a pre-filled message)
- **Post on Discord** … Copy a Discord-ready message to your clipboard

### Edit key
The first time you publish, FAMITORY generates an 8-digit **edit key**. You'll need it to **edit your game from a different phone or PC**, so be sure to write it down.

<img src="assets/screenshots/エディットキー.png" alt="Edit key" style="max-width:40%; min-width:208px; display:block; margin:16px 0; border:none; box-shadow:none; border-radius:0;">

### Updating / unpublishing
Once published, a "Published" label appears.

<img src="assets/screenshots/公開中ラベル.png" alt="Published label" style="max-width:40%; min-width:208px; display:block; margin:16px 0; border:none; box-shadow:none; border-radius:0;">

Even after publishing, press **"Update Game"** to replace it with the latest version while keeping the same URL.

If you want to take it down, press **"Unpublish"** and no one will be able to access it anymore. You can republish it any time at the same URL — your likes and high scores will still be there.
