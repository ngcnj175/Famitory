# &lt;Chapter 2&gt; Set Your Game Info

Before you start making a game, decide the **title**, **author name**, and **edit key**.
Setting them early makes saving and sharing easier!

> *Screenshot coming soon*

---

## Title

The name of your game. It shows up at the top of the PLAY screen and is what other people see when you share it.

## Author name

The name of the person who made this game. It appears as the creator when you publish.

## Edit key

A password-like key that keeps others from overwriting your game.
Be sure to remember it!
