# 8. Save & Open (SAVE / OPEN)

You can save the games you make, open them later to keep working, and even carry them to another device.

## Save

Press **SAVE** to store the game you're working on inside your browser. Save often — whenever you reach a good stopping point.

- **Tap SAVE** … Opens the save screen, where you can name and save your game, or export the data.
- **Long-press SAVE** … Overwrites the game you're working on right away, without opening the save screen (shortcut).

**Export**<br>
You can also export your game as a file. Use this when you want to move it to another device, or keep a backup.

!> If you clear your browser history or don't use FAMITORY for a while, your SAVE data may be lost. For games you really want to keep, **export them as a file, or publish them with SHARE!**

---

## Open

Press the **OPEN** button to see the list of saved games, then pick the one you want to play or edit.

You can also:

- **Copy** … Duplicate the game (the original stays as it is)
- **Delete** … Permanently remove the game (this cannot be undone, so be careful!)

**Import**<br>
Load a file you exported before and add it to your game list. Use this when you want to continue a game you made on another device, or restore from a backup.
